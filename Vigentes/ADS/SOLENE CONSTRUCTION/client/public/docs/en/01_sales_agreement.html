1. Agreement and Authorization

By signing this Sales Agreement, the Customer authorizes Solene Cabinets & Countertops ("Company") to proceed with the fabrication, delivery, and installation of the products described herein. No verbal agreements are recognized. Everything must be in writing on this contract. Any modifications to this agreement must be made in writing and signed by both parties to be valid and enforceable.

2. Measurements and Specifications

The salesperson's measurements and specifications are approximate and are not to be relied upon as exact. The Company will send a professional installer to confirm all measurements prior to fabrication. The Customer is responsible for verifying all specifications at the time of signing. The Company is not responsible for errors resulting from Customer-provided measurements or specifications.

3. Manufacturing and Lead Times

Cabinet orders from time to time come in damaged, manufactured wrong, or ordered incorrectly. All re-orders can take up to 2 to 3 weeks to be manufactured and shipped. Our goal is to complete your project in less than 7 to 10 working days. In the event of re-orders, this time can be extended. The Company will make every effort to complete your kitchen as quickly as possible to minimize inconvenience.

4. Installation Schedule

Installation start time is approximately 8 to 14 weeks after approval of measure. Scheduling and/or HOA approval delays are not allowed to change these times. The Company will call as soon as possible to schedule your job. If you are using our financing, the clock doesn't start ticking until your loan is approved. If the start of your installation exceeds past the estimated time above, the Company will credit your account $50.00 per week for every week that we fall behind. This contract cannot be altered after the date of the measure.

5. Cancellation Policy

Buyer's Right to Cancel: This is a home solicitation sale, and if you do not want the goods or services, you may cancel this agreement by providing written notice to the Company in person, by telegram, or by mail. This notice must indicate that you do not want the goods or services and must be delivered or postmarked before midnight of the third business day after you sign this agreement. If you cancel, the Company will return all deposits and any cash down payment within 10 business days.

6. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer is responsible for ensuring that the job site is accessible and ready for installation on the scheduled date. The Customer must ensure that all appliances (sinks, faucets, stove, cooktop, dishwasher) are at the job site on the day of the template and installation. A trip charge will apply for additional visits caused by Customer's failure to have the site ready. The Customer must ensure that all plumbing and gas lines are disconnected prior to installation. Final wall preparation (painting, wallpaper, tile, etc.) should not be completed prior to the installation of new countertops, as incidental damage such as scrapes, dents, or punctures may occur and are the Customer's responsibility to repair.

7. Lead Paint Practices

The Customer hereby acknowledges receipt of a copy of the pamphlet "Renovate Right: Important Lead Hazard Information for Families, Child Care Providers and Schools" informing the Customer of the potential risk of lead hazard exposure from renovation activity to be performed in the Customer's home. The Customer has received this pamphlet before the work began.

8. Warranty

Solene Cabinets & Countertops provides a one (1) year limited warranty on all installation services performed by the Company. This warranty covers defects in workmanship and installation only. It does not cover damage caused by misuse, abuse, neglect, accidents, or normal wear and tear. The warranty is valid only for the original Customer and is non-transferable. See the separate Warranty document for full terms and conditions.

9. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall first be submitted to mediation before resorting to litigation. The parties agree to attempt to resolve disputes in good faith. If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Orange County, Florida. The prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

The Company's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total contract price paid by the Customer.
