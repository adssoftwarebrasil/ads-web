Template & Installation Instructions
Important guidelines for a successful countertop installation
Template
  • The Customer or responsible project coordinator MUST be present during the entire template process. There are many details that need to be confirmed, and someone must be available to answer all questions. A signature will be required after the template is completed. We reserve the right to reschedule a template if nobody is home to make such decisions. A trip charge may apply.
  • All appliances MUST be at the job site on the day of the template (Sinks, Faucets, Stove/Cooktop, Downdraft, Dishwasher).
    — If you have an Apron/Farmhouse sink, please make sure it is in place and leveled prior to the template.
    — If you purchased any accessories with the sink, please make sure you hand them to us to ensure proper fit.
  • Cabinets need to be completely installed and leveled. Make sure all end panels are installed.
  • If the customer is keeping old cabinets and only replacing the old countertops, please make sure the old tops are completely removed before we arrive. IF WE ARE REMOVING THE OLD COUNTERTOPS, please make sure you tell the homeowner to remove everything from the old tops before we get there. MAKE SURE all plumbing is disconnected; gas lines also need to be disconnected.
  • Make sure all windows around the countertop area are permanently installed.
  • Make sure half walls (if any) are completed and leveled.
  • Final wall preparation (painting, wallpaper, tile, etc.) should not be completed prior to the installation of new countertops. Our installers are very careful, but incidental damage such as scrapes, dents, or punctures may occur, and it is the customer's responsibility to repair them.
Installation
  • The Customer or responsible project coordinator MUST be present during the entire installation process. After the project is installed, we need a responsible person to inspect the countertops and provide a signature.
  • MAKE SURE all faucets are at the job site.
  • MAKE SURE the stove, slide-in range, or cooktop/downdraft are at the job site to ensure proper fit.
  • MAKE SURE the dishwasher is IN PLACE so we can properly secure it to the countertop.
  • Please help us get these jobs done in one trip. A TRIP CHARGE will apply for additional visits.
  • If we are doing a Full Height Backsplash, it will be measured the same day we install the countertops. Please make sure the upper cabinets, microwave, and hood are permanently installed. Make sure all electrical outlets and switches are permanently in place.
Important: Please make sure all plumbing and gas lines are disconnected before our installation team arrives. Solene Cabinets & Countertops is not responsible for any damage caused by connected plumbing or gas lines during installation.

Care & Maintenance of Natural Stones
Template & Installation Instructions — Page 2 of 2
  • Natural stones may be cleaned with stone cleaner or a mild dish soap. Make sure it is non-acidic and ammonia-free.
    NOTE: Lemon-based and orange-based cleaners are acidic — do not use them. Never use an abrasive cleaner.
  • We apply a first layer of sealer on all countertops that we manufacture at our fabrication facility. Our installation crew will add another coat of sealer to the countertop after they install the top. (It is the customer's responsibility to maintain sealing the countertops after they are installed.) We recommend limestone, marble, quartzite, and lighter granite colors to be sealed once every six months; darker granite colors once a year. Soapstone does not need to be sealed — simply apply mineral oil as often as desired.
  • Natural stones can stain if spills are left unattended for a period of time. Some items that can cause staining are oils, wine, fruit juices, and other acidic products. Spills should be cleaned up quickly with mild detergent and water.
  • Do not place hot pots or pans directly on natural stone countertops. Always use trivets or heat pads to protect the surface.
  • Do not use your countertop as a cutting board. Always use a cutting board to protect both the countertop and your knives.
  • Avoid using harsh chemicals, bleach, or abrasive cleaning products on natural stone surfaces, as they can damage the finish and void the warranty.
Quartz Countertop Care
  • Quartz countertops are highly durable and require minimal maintenance. Clean with a mild soap and warm water using a soft cloth.
  • Avoid exposing quartz to strong chemicals such as paint removers, oven cleaners, or products containing trichloroethane or methylene chloride.
  • Although quartz is heat-resistant, always use trivets or hot pads when placing hot items on the surface to prevent thermal shock.
  • Quartz does not require sealing. However, avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight, as UV rays can cause discoloration over time.
