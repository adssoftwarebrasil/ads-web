Cabinets & Countertops Installation Services

SOLENE CABINETS & COUNTERTOPS will warrant your cabinets and countertops against any fabrication and/or installation defect during the first year after initial installation, limited to indoor installation only, provided that Solene Cabinets & Countertops performs the installation. Solene Cabinets & Countertops will not provide any warranty service if the product shows evidence that it has been tampered with, misused, abused, or altered, including chemical abuse (e.g., oil stains) and physical abuse (e.g., excessive chipped edges). This warranty does not cover damage caused by items out of our control, such as settlement, fire, or acts of nature.

The cabinets supporting the installed countertops are the client's responsibility, and any cracks or breakages caused by not having enough support, corbels, cleats, etc. are not covered by this warranty. Solene Cabinets & Countertops assumes no liability for cracking countertop material installed on inadequately braced or supporting cabinets.

Solene Cabinets & Countertops is limited to the repair or replacement of the defective piece only, subject to the availability of the material color. If the color is no longer available, liability is limited to the cash value of the installed piece based on current square foot prices, if we determine that a satisfactory repair cannot be made.

This warranty does not cover natural characteristics of stone such as color variations, water lines, surface marks, or natural aging. Solene Cabinets & Countertops has no control over the existence of any natural irregularities found in the natural stone and consequently assumes no liability for any fissures, inconsistencies, or variations in color and patterns found in the finished product originating from the raw material. Solene Cabinets & Countertops makes no warranties, express or implied, as to stain or imperviousness of the products installed.

Warranty Exclusions
This warranty does not cover:
  • Damage resulting from misuse, abuse, neglect, accidents, or unauthorized modifications
  • Normal wear and tear, including minor scratches and surface dulling
  • Damage caused by harsh chemicals, abrasive cleaners, or improper cleaning methods
  • Damage caused by excessive heat, including placing hot pots or pans directly on surfaces
  • Damage resulting from structural movement, settling, or inadequate cabinet support
  • Pre-existing conditions or damage present before installation
  • Damage caused by third-party contractors or unauthorized repairs
  • Natural stone color variations, veining, or other inherent characteristics
  • Outdoor installations or installations in areas with excessive moisture or humidity
Warranty Claim Process

To make a warranty claim, the Customer must contact Solene Cabinets & Countertops at (689) 331-4029 within the warranty period. The Customer must provide proof of purchase and a description of the defect. Solene Cabinets & Countertops will inspect the issue and determine if it is covered under this warranty. Warranty service will be scheduled at a mutually convenient time. This warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original purchaser.
